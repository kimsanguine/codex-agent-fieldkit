# Public Surface Policy

This repository keeps public, synthetic learning material in `guides/` and
starter-kit working artifacts in each kit's `workspace/` directory.

- Any `docs` or `.archive` directory at any depth is local/private and must not
  be committed.
- Public examples remain fictional or synthetic; no client data, credentials,
  internal links, or private screenshots belong here.
- `scripts/check_public_surface.sh` enforces this policy in `make validate`.
